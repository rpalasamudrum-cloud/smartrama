Ramayana

Ayodhya and Lanka, the exile and the war — Rama, Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, Ravana.

Ten points for the easy, fifteen for the middling, twenty-five for the difficult — seventy-five in a round.
The five of the day are the same five for every reader. The unlimited rounds are yours alone.
